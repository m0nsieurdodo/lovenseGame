# AIM TRAINER - Lovense Arcade

## GAMEPLAY
- cibles apparaissent aléatoirement
- clic pour les toucher
- timer 60 secondes

---

## SCORING
- score = hits
- combo multiplier

---

## LOVENSE
- hit → 0.2s vibration (10–20%)
- combo x10 → vibration continue légère
- combo x25 → vibration forte pulsée
- miss → baisse légère intensité

---

## TECH
- setInterval spawn targets
- click handlers
- state React léger

---

## UI
- timer
- score
- combo counter
- feedback visuel hit
