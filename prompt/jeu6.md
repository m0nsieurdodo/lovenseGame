# BREAKOUT - Lovense Arcade

## GAMEPLAY
- paddle en bas
- briques en haut
- balle rebondit

---

## LOVENSE
- brique cassée → 10–20% vibration (0.2s)
- combo 10 → vibration continue 50%
- combo 20 → 80% forte vibration
- game over → stop

---

## TECH
- canvas + requestAnimationFrame
- collision detection simple
- React state minimal
